FAQ

Is this an official Hacker News app?

No. is an independent, unofficial client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Hacker News or Y Combinator.

Is my Hacker News login safe?

Chronicle is a device only, offline Hacker News reader. Credentials are not entered.

Do you collect analytics?

None. No analytics, no crash data, no tracking.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email with a clear description. Feature requests welcome.
